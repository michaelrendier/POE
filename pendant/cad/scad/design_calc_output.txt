COARSE SERVO vs REQUIREMENT at 1.22 m: one servo step = 1534 urad (= 0.088 deg) -> 1.87 mm at the target;
  tolerance w0/(4d) = 10 urad  ->  the servo step is 152x too coarse by itself. A fine stage is REQUIRED.
FINE STAGE (lateral fibre-tip shift behind the lens, angle = shift / f): to cover one servo step needs +-0.038 mm of shift;
  to resolve 3 urad needs 126 nm steps: piezo-class, not a hobby actuator.

FOCUS (axial fibre-tip shift): about 8 mm of travel covers 0.3 m to 1.5 m for f = 50 mm; resolution needed ~ z_R / 20 at the far end.

PHASE: path from each arm to a point changes by up to ~1.2 m as the point moves: the phase actuator must wrap modulo 2 pi;
  keeping 96% of the peak needs about lambda/30 = 21 nm of path error per arm; mechanical metrology cannot give that over a metre,
  so phase has to be closed on a signal at the focus (fog-lit point -> pinhole photodiode), not set from geometry alone.
AIR: dn/dT ~ 1e-6 per K, so 1.2 m of path moves 1200 nm per K; a still-air tent and a phase loop of ~1 kHz or better are assumed.

POWER at the test: 13 arms x 75 uW = 0.98 mW total (about class 2); 18 arms x 75 uW = 1.35 mW (check the class: 18 x 75 uW exceeds 1 mW).
